## 1. Passes as specified

- Unit of every split, null and rate is the cell (3.2.1): whole cells held out, `_assert_grouped` on `cell_id` for every fold list and on `workload_key` for every workload-grouped list. This is protocol 1.1 and 3.8 as written.
- LOWO over all workloads of both classes (3.2.2 `fold_lowo`): one fold per `workload_key`, all reps held out, sandbox folds give the TPR and benign folds the FPR, `lowo/final` scores the stage-3 `test_only` rows and never trains on them. Protocol 1.3 and P3 D5 exactly.
- The in-fold threshold (3.3.2, 3.3.3): the 95th and 99th percentiles of the benign training cells' out-of-bag scores, strict exceedance, realized out-of-fold FPR beside the TPR, the pooled-curve reading labelled `POST_HOC_LABEL`, G-CAL deciding whether the two agree within the null's spread. Protocol 1.6 items 2 and 3, 3.7, and 4.3 G-CAL. (But see item 2.1 below: the rule is only honest when a row is a cell.)
- The workload-level null (3.3.4): the S sandbox labels reassigned to a random S-subset of the S + B workloads with cells inheriting, exhaustive below 500, `NULL_NOT_ESTIMABLE` below 20 assignments with no verb, strict exceedance of p95 with ties failing, rank reported, `test_only` never in the pool, a smoke run of fewer than 500 draws refused by name. Protocol 1.4, 2.2 and CR3 2.1 exactly. `count_assignments(8, 13) = 203,490` and the level-2 count 280 are right.
- Per-member recall in eighths (3.3.3 `per_member`, Table 8): the S strings `k/n` with `n` reduced by the member's at-floor cells, min, median and max but no mean. Protocol 2.4 and P3 D5.
- G-OP's numbers and refusal (3.5.5): five cells from three distinct benign workloads, else `GOP_SET_BY_FEW` with the families named and the TPR carrying no verb; both readings of "the cells behind the rate" (threshold setters, realized false positives) exposed and both counts written. Protocol 2.3 and 4.3. (The counting unit needs one correction, item 2.3.)
- G-SIG (3.5.8), G-FP (3.5.9) and G-1C (3.3.6, 3.5.10) implement CR3 2.16, 2.15 and 2.19 as I defined them, with al-Kindi's overrule (one-class standing at every S) honoured and the isolation-forest-versus-density question put to the author rather than decided silently.
- G-N (3.5.3), G-L (i) (3.5.4), G-LM with the factor-two band and the per-member refusal (3.5.6), the majority baseline and random scorer (3.3.3), G-M with the exact one-sided binomial over non-tied units (3.5.13). One correction to my own text: protocol 2.5 and CR3 2.7 say "at least eight with one worsening (p = 0.035)"; the exact value for eight up and one down is 0.0195, and 0.035 is seven up and one down of eight. The spec's `exact_sign_test` computes the probability rather than copying the count table, which is the right repair.
- The leak tests that exist: the order test within class with the half label as a per-workload constant, its workload-level null and the drift regression (3.5.7; protocol 3.2); G-ANCHOR (i) through `gx` and (ii) on the idle sets with early-against-late idle (protocol 3.1, 3.3); the pre-declared exclusion list in every `params` block (protocol 3.4); the alias falsifier on `dt_est_s` (CR3 2.26). The synthetic pass and refuse pairs for order (`--order-confound off|on`, `--drift-level`) and for the launch label (`round_robin|confounded`, `--idle-campaigns 1|2`) are well formed.
- Class imbalance: `CLASS_WEIGHT = None` declared before the data with the stage-1 ratio 104 : 64 at the cell level; protocol 2.1 and 2.6 say no reweighting at about 2 : 1, and the workload count, not the cell count, is the constraint. `S = 8` clears G-N's headline count and G-M's five-unit floor.
- Hyperparameters declared as constants and written into `params`; (W, H) inherited per rung and never per class; `relaunched_grouping = "parent"` (CR3 2.31) is the correct default from the split-leakage side (protocol 3.8: a re-launched kernel is the same program, so it shares the key).

## 2. Must change before build

### 2.1 The rows are windows; the sources say the rows are cells (3.1.1, 3.2.1, 3.3.2, section 7 items 8 and 9)

What is wrong. `make_detection_labels` returns one row per window, the forest is fitted on about 39,000 window rows, and the cell's score is an aggregation rule chosen in section 7 item 8. Protocol 1.1 ("the learner sees one row per cell per rung; windows feed only the temporal gates"), al-Kindi 1.4 and 2.2 ("windows never become rows"; "the declared per-cell summaries") and CR3 1.5 all fix the row as the cell. The spec inherits epoch 1's window rows because that is what `run_split_stage` does for the encoding paper, and then reads ML 1.6 as silent on "how windows become a cell". It is not silent; the row is the cell.

Why it matters beyond the letter. The in-fold threshold is the 95th percentile of the benign training cells' out-of-bag scores. Under window rows a training cell has about 230 half-overlapping windows; every tree that scores one of them out of bag was trained on roughly 145 of its siblings, so a benign cell's out-of-bag score is close to its in-sample score, the threshold sits far below where unseen-workload benign cells score, and the realized FPR at "5 percent" is unbounded by the rule. Section 7 item 9 concedes exactly this. A threshold that the procedure cannot hold is not an in-fold threshold; it is a number with a label.

Exact correction. Add `ROW_UNIT = "cell"` to 3.3.1 (section 7 item). `make_detection_labels` collapses the loaded feature file to one row per admissible classed cell using `models.cell_vectors`' rule (the mean over the cell's windows of the window features at the inherited grid point, `nanmean`, the same vector `gv_two_class`, `harness`, `alias_detection` and epoch 1's clustering already use); feature names and `d` are unchanged, so G-DIM is unchanged. Every fold function, `run_operating_point`, `oob_cell_scores`, `in_fold_threshold`, `quarantine_l1_two_class`, `run_one_class`, `run_level2`, `run_level3` and the ladder (after `build_prefix_features`) run on cell rows; `cell_scores` becomes the identity and `SCORE_AGGREGATION` is written as `not applicable: row unit is the cell`; the majority vote of levels 2 and 3 becomes the row's argmax. `ROW_UNIT = "window"` stays as the declared alternative for the author, and under it `THRESHOLD_SOURCE = "oob"` is refused in `scores.json` (`not applicable: out-of-bag threshold under window rows (sibling windows in bag)`) and `inner_lowo` is the only admissible source. Recompute the cost table of 6.2 by timing one fit on the synthetic corpus; fits on about 160 rows are a different order of magnitude, which also makes the LOCO null (item 2.7) and `inner_lowo` affordable.

### 2.2 `in_denominator` is undefined for the `control` verdict and for a pending G-K0 (3.3.3, 3.3.7)

What is wrong. The rule covers "positives above floor", benign cells and at-floor positives. Under the null, idle workloads are relabelled positive in a fraction of the assignments and carry the verdict `control`; the spec does not say whether they enter the null's denominator. A cell with `floor_verdict = "pending: gk0 not run"` likewise has no rule, so a TPR could be printed over an undefined denominator.

Exact correction. In `summarize_operating_point`: `control` counts as at floor (the idle cells define the floor and are undetectable by construction), in the observed run and under every permutation alike; when any positive cell's verdict is `pending: gk0 not run`, every `tpr_*` field is `not run: gk0 not run` and no number is written. Add both to the 4.5 table as tested outcomes.

### 2.3 G-OP counts the threshold setters as a union over folds (3.5.5)

What is wrong. "The cells behind the declared rate" is a per-fold quantity: each fold has one threshold set by the top five or six of its benign training cells. The union over 21 folds can reach five cells from three workloads while every single fold's threshold is set by one workload, which is the case the gate exists to name.

Exact correction. Under `cells_rule = "threshold_setters"`, count per fold and roll up as the worst fold (`GOP_SUPPORTED` only when every fold's setters number at least `min_cells` from at least `min_workloads` workloads); write `n_folds_unsupported` and the union as a disclosure. Under `realized_fps` the pooled count stands. Run `gop` on `loco` and `lofo` as well as `lowo`: on stage 1 the LOFO fold that holds out the kernels sets its threshold on eight idle cells of one workload, so its flag columns must carry `GOP_SET_BY_FEW` by construction rather than a number that looks like an operating point.

### 2.4 B1-G3 and the L1 baseline (3.3.5, 4.5, Table 7)

What is wrong, in four parts. (a) The one-feature model's decision is a training-accuracy split compared against the forest's flag at 5 percent FPR: two different operating points, so a disagreement can come from the point and not from the feature. (b) The tables print the re-run without the quarantined feature as the headline (SPEC 3.7.2's rule). In the encoding paper that rule guarded against a level column acting as the label; here the level is handled by normalization, G-L and G-LM, and metadata and cadence are excluded by declaration, so what B1-G3 can still catch on the declared matrix is a behavioural feature that alone separates the classes, which protocol 1.5 says is the result ("if L1 matches the forest, that is the result and the better one"). Printing the re-run as the headline would delete a legitimate one-axis detection. (c) The L1 baseline row of protocol 1.5 (the best single feature under the same LOWO) is absent from `scores.json` and Table 7. (d) The 4.5 pair is not a known answer: on the synthetic corpus members 1 to 4 are separable on `r_l0_q50_per` alone by construction, so the reduced corpus on `content` should quarantine that feature, not return an empty quarantine.

Exact correction. Define the one-feature model at the operating point: per fold, the direction is the sign of the difference between the sandbox and benign training medians of the feature and the threshold is the `1 - fpr` quantile of the benign training cells' feature values in that direction (in-fold, no fitting on held-out cells); its per-cell flag is compared with the forest's `flag_05`. The same run yields the L1 row: for every feature its TPR at the in-fold threshold, realized FPR and AUC under LOWO, the best feature chosen per fold on the training fold by training AUC, with its own null from the same subsets; `scores.json["l1"]` and a Table 7 row `l1 (best single feature)` naming the feature. Table 7 prints the full-feature forest as the headline, the quarantined feature in the B1-G3 column, and the re-run's TPR beside it (`score_source = "full"`; `with_quarantine` kept). Give the synthetic corpus a switch (for example `--conjunction-members`, members 1 to 4 separable only by two features jointly, each alone inside the benign range) as the empty-quarantine case, and make the default corpus the quarantine case with the feature named.

### 2.5 At-floor positive cells stay in every training set (3.2.2, 3.3.3)

What is wrong. A member whose cells are at floor leaves the denominator (K3 F3) but its 8 cells still train every other fold as positives, teaching the forest that the idle band is the sandbox class. That is B1's forest lesson (protocol 3.6, environment recognition), it raises the idle cells' out-of-bag scores and with them every threshold, and it manufactures an idle false-positive rate that G-FP would then report as a finding about the lead. The definition is silent on training, so this is a choice the spec has made without declaring it.

Exact correction. Add `AT_FLOOR_POSITIVES_IN_TRAINING = False` (my recommended default; a parameter listed in section 7, the author decides): positive cells whose verdict is at floor, at harness floor or `control` are removed from every training set in the observed run and under every permutation (the fold's `n_train_cells` records it); the idle cells stay in training as benign. `True` keeps the current behaviour. Write the value into `params` and Table 7's note.

### 2.6 The cadence and active-fraction probes are missing (3.1.1, 3.5.14; protocol 3.4 and 3.5)

What is wrong. The exclusion by declaration is recorded but never tested, and the two "Test" lines of protocol 3.4 and 3.5 (a one-feature model on pair count alone, and on the fraction of snapshots inside the idle band, under LOWO with the workload-level null) are not built. On stage 1 the sandbox cells were captured in one block after the kernels under their own launch label, so cadence is the cheapest way for the class to become audible.

Exact correction. Add `gates_detection.leak_probe`: for each of `n_pairs`, `dt_est_s` (sidecars), `frac_above_band` and `K_med` (`gk0_cells.csv`), the one-feature model of 2.4 under LOWO with the workload-level null; `leak_probe.csv` rows `rung-free, quantity, auc, null_p95, rank, verdict` with `LEAK_AUDIBLE` / `LEAK_NOT_AUDIBLE` (new vocabulary in 3.0), read as a size in Table 9 (row `cadence` and row `active fraction`); K_med audible is not a leak but is printed beside G-L. Synthetic pair: the default corpus (constant `--n-pairs`) reads not audible; a new switch `--sandbox-n-pairs N` (the member cells at a different pair count) reads audible. Add a test asserting that every feature name in every npz is in `series.feature_names(rung, normalized)` and that none of `excluded_by_declaration` is a feature.

### 2.7 The one-class run has no null (3.3.6, 3.5.10, Table 11)

What is wrong. F9 ("one-class inside the null while LOWO above it") and Table 11's note need the one-class null; `run_one_class` and the `one-class` CLI have no `n_perm`.

Exact correction. Add `n_perm: int = N_PERM` to `run_one_class` and `--null-perm` to the CLI; the null is the same workload-level subsets as LOWO (same seed), each permutation refitting the per-benign-workload folds and the final model on the permuted benign set; statistics `tpr05` and `auc`; `null.json` written in the split directory; `g1c.csv` gains `null_verdict`. Under cell rows this is 14 isolation-forest fits per permutation and cheap. With the same cheapness, make `--null-splits lowo,loco` the default so G-SIG can refuse (section 7 item 11 currently leaves it unable to).

### 2.8 The rep-identity disclosure for LOCO is missing (3.5.15, Table 8; protocol 3.5)

What is wrong. A member whose reps are not repeats (a shared seed or a deterministic program) has L0 near zero, and its LOCO row is then within-trace, not a signature ceiling; nothing labels it.

Exact correction. `gv_two_class` writes per member `L0_member` and `L0_ratio = L0_member / median over benign workloads of L0_b` (over the features, the median ratio); Table 8's LOCO row prints the ratio under each member; a member below `REPS_IDENTICAL_RATIO` (parameter, section 7, default proposed 0.1, the author's) carries `reps near identical: LOCO reads as within-trace` in the note.

## 3. For the author

1. The row unit (2.1). Cell is what you decided in D5 and what the council wrote; window is epoch 1's habit. If you keep windows anywhere, the out-of-bag threshold is not admissible there and `inner_lowo` is the rule.
2. The cell vector: the mean over windows (default, epoch 1's rule) or the mean plus the 5th, 50th and 95th percentiles over windows (`d` triples; G-DIM then matches on the new count). The mean loses the within-cell tail; the quantiles keep it at the cost of dimension on about 160 rows.
3. At-floor positives out of training (2.5): my recommendation is out; the alternative keeps them and reads any idle false positives as a finding about the lead.
4. Threshold source under cell rows: `oob` follows the definition's letter and is optimistic only through sibling reps (the realized FPR is the measurement); `inner_lowo` is LOWO-honest and now affordable (13 extra fits per fold). `threshold_quantile_method = "higher"` is the conservative percentile.
5. The null's verdict statistic: P3 D5 puts the verb on the TPR at 5 percent, my protocol on the AUC because the TPR null is quantized in steps of 1/56 with ties failing. Both are computed; choose which carries the verb, and read the other's rank beside it.
6. One-class primary: the isolation forest is not a density model; a full-covariance GMM on `combined` (d = 60) with about 100 benign cell rows is singular, so if you want the density model the declaration must be `GaussianMixture(covariance_type="diag")` or a GMM after a declared PCA, and it must be primary now.
7. LOFO on stage 1 under `kernel_family_rule = "tier"` has one benign family in training when the kernels are held out (eight idle cells against 64 sandbox cells, 8 : 1 the other way); its FPR on the kernels will be high by construction and its threshold is `GOP_SET_BY_FEW`. Consider a separate `lofo_family_rule = "archetype"` (five folds) as the stage-1 reading while G-FP and the per-family recall keep `tier`.
8. G-OP's roll-up (2.3): worst fold, or a declared fraction of folds; I propose worst fold.
9. `REPS_IDENTICAL_RATIO` (2.8) and the cadence-probe vocabulary (2.6) are new parameters with proposed defaults; confirm or change them before the data.
10. Stage 1 cannot separate class from launch label or from position by any test in this layer (the order test is within class; G-ANCHOR (ii) has one idle set), which P3 0a already states as a limitation; the cadence probe of 2.6 is the one stage-1 handle on that leak and should be in the validity block.
