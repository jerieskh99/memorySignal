Six weeks later, he threw the detector away

A conference draft written six weeks after the defence. It keeps the measurement apparatus and deletes the thing the thesis was named for — no autoencoder, no malware detection. This is the hinge the whole rest of the project turns on.

The one idea to hold onto

When you cannot defend the conclusion, you can still defend the instrument. He dropped the claim and kept the substrate — and immediately started asking a question the thesis never had: is the sampling even real?

1
The deletion

The paper that removed its own headline

The thesis was titled for a capability: detecting anomalies by fingerprinting memory. Six weeks after defending it, the follow-up paper presents the acquisition and representation layer alone as the contribution.1

Gone: the autoencoder. Gone: the malware detection claim. What remains is the part that turns a running machine into a stream of numbers — and an argument that that is the thing worth publishing.

Takeaway

Nobody asked for this. The thesis passed with the highest grade available. Six weeks later its author quietly withdrew its central claim and kept the plumbing — which is a judgement about what he actually believed, made when there was no external pressure to make it.

Why the timing matters

Entry 2's results chapter already contained the sentence "this performance is too good to be true." The retreat is that sentence taking six weeks to finish its thought.

2
Formalisation

Writing memory down as an object

The thesis described its pipeline. The paper defines it. Memory becomes a formal mapping — an address and a time index to a block of bits — and the whole capture becomes a formal three-dimensional signal: pages by snapshots by content.1

And the transform that produces the difference is named for exactly what Entry 1 already argued: a function "inspired by the concept of a discrete derivative."

In plain terms

The intuition from the thesis — I am holding the derivative of a state signal — stops being a motivating analogy and becomes a definition. That is the difference between a metaphor that justifies a toolkit and a claim you can be wrong about.

Where this earns its keep

Once memory is an object with a stated shape, you can ask whether a given capture is one — whether it satisfies the definition. That question does not exist in the thesis. Everything from Entry 5 onward is that question being asked over and over.

3
The seed

The sentence that seeded two years of work

The paper adds something the thesis had nothing equivalent to: an analysis of what sets the sampling rate. It weighs the processor's clock, the operating system's scheduler tick, and an external timer — and picks the external timer, "to maintain forensic integrity."1

Then it makes the claim that everything afterwards is a reckoning with:

IEEE conference draft, 2025-03-21

Pausing the machine makes each snapshot "an instantaneous, zero-duration sampling event," ensuring "each snapshot forms a mathematically valid discrete signal."

It is the right thing to worry about, and the reasoning is sound. It is also, at this point, entirely unverified — a statement about what the code ought to do.

Be honest about the limits

Nothing in this paper measures the thing it asserts. The gap between "this design makes the sampling valid" and "the sampling was measured and is valid" is fourteen months wide, and the answer when it finally arrives is no.

4
The mis-prediction

The plan predicted the opposite of what happened

The thesis has a Future Work section. It is a scaling-up agenda: hybrid feature stacks, GPU acceleration for real-time operation, extension to network traffic, dedicated hardware, recurrent and attention models, more malware families.2

Almost none of it happened. What happened was the reverse:

The plan saidWhat actually followed
More models — LSTM, attentionThe model was removed entirely
More speed — GPUs, real timeDeliberately slower, controlled capture
More malware, more varietyNo real malware at all; safe synthetic workloads
A richer feature stackA single scalar, and the question of whether it suffices
Broader deploymentTwo years spent on whether one machine measures correctly
Takeaway

The retreat was not a plan. Nothing written in January 2025 anticipates it. Each step downward happened because the floor gave way — and the written Future Work is the best evidence that it was discovered rather than designed.

5
The shape

Four retreats, none of them planned

Seen from here, the whole project has one motion. Every time a claim could not hold, the response was not to defend it but to go one level lower and secure the thing underneath.

The fourth level is still being written

By April 2026 the framing is that the contribution is an architectural form — what any valid realisation must preserve, expressed as roles, invariants and conditions of valid observation. The working test for whether a sentence belongs to the form: "could a different implementation still satisfy this?"3

That paper is not finished. It is listed here because it is the current top of the stack, not because it is done.

What this reframes

Read this way, the engineering disasters in the entries that follow stop being a list of bugs. Each one is a condition of valid observation being violated and then repaired — sampling that wasn't uniform, a role separation that had collapsed, contextual identity destroyed by scratch files in the wrong place. Same story, seven times.

Part Two starts on 4 November 2025, with a repository whose README says it exists only to check that the arithmetic is self-consistent. That modesty is the whole point.

Glossary

Hover any dotted term for its definition.

Three-dimensional signal
Memory redefined as one formal object rather than a folder of files — an address, a time index, and a content value, all three treated as axes of the same array. Once it has a stated shape, a real capture either satisfies that shape or it doesn't.
Discrete derivative
The difference between one sample and the next, treated as the rate of change of a signal that only exists at discrete moments in time — the memory equivalent of a continuous function's derivative.
Sampling rate
How often a signal is measured. Every frequency computed downstream is only meaningful relative to the true rate the samples were actually taken at — which is exactly what turns out to be wrong, later in this diary.
Architectural form
What any valid version of a system must preserve — its roles, its invariants, the conditions under which its output means anything — as distinct from one particular implementation of it. The test for whether a sentence belongs to the form: could a different implementation still satisfy it?
