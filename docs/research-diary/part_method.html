What carries forward

Everything before this was one project. This entry is what is left when the memory-signal subject is stripped away — the handful of moves that produced every result and caught every mistake, stated so they could be carried into work that has nothing to do with memory.

The one idea to hold onto

The transferable thing in two years of work is not the signal, the pipeline, or the detector. It is a way of not being fooled — and it is small enough to write down.

1
The reflex

A perfect score is a symptom

The move: when a number looks too good, treat it as a report of a bug in the measurement until proven otherwise. Do not celebrate it. Go find what it is made of.

Entry 2 (2025) and Entry 8 (2026) — the same reflex, sixteen months apart

In the thesis: 100% detection, and the results chapter itself says "this performance is too good to be true and likely indicates a bias." In the 2026 review: 100% classification, and the verdict is "a red flag, not a triumph." Both were followed, not filed. The first was a degenerate model; the second was one leaked feature.

Entries 2 and 8.

The rule, portable

High performance on a hard problem is a hypothesis about a leak, not evidence of success. The first question is always "what could produce this number without the thing I want being true?"

2
The inversion

When everything fails the test, suspect the test

The move: a check that fails uniformly, or passes uniformly, is more likely broken than right. Turn the instrument on itself before you trust its verdict on the data.

Where it caught a false negative

A parameter sweep returned "no winner" for every option. The gate was scoring on a number that didn't depend on the parameter being swept — it could not discriminate in principle. (Entry 7.)

Where it caught a false positive

A gate passed all 132 cells. It was reading a field that didn't exist, and "missing" fell through to "pass." Every cell had actually failed. (Entry 8.)

The same shape appears at least four times across the record — a health check that tested the wrong signal, a preflight that reported a pass on one workload of a hundred, a checker that called healthy captures corrupt. The lesson is not "checks are unreliable." It is that a check is itself an experiment, and needs its own null result: run it where you know the answer, and confirm it says so.

The rule, portable

Before trusting a verdict, feed the verdict-maker a case whose answer you already know. A tool that cannot fail on bad input cannot be believed on good input.

3
The constraint-first choice

Budget the design by what the data can support

The move: choose the method by counting what you can afford to estimate, not by reaching for the most powerful tool. Sophistication you cannot feed is a liability.

Entry 8 — the detector selection

Five change-point detectors were on the table. The deciding question was not "which is most accurate" but "how many cells do we have for hyperparameter selection?" — about twenty. So: "anything with more than one knob is over-parameterised for our data." The winner was the one with a single knob. The Bayesian option needed a prior, the penalty method a cost model, the network a training set that did not exist.

Entry 8; the same logic set the replicate count and the "no pooled score" rule.

The rule, portable

Count your degrees of freedom before you choose your model. The right complexity is the most the evidence can constrain, and not one knob more.

4
The calibration instinct

Set the bar from measured noise, not convention

The move: before accepting a threshold, run the metric on deliberate nonsense and see what it scores. An acceptance bar that isn't anchored to the noise floor is decoration.

Entry 6 (the principle) and Entry 8 (the payoff)

The planned bar was a literature convention, 0.8. Run on noise, the scorer returned 0.41–0.62 — so 0.8 was noise plus a margin nobody had measured. The objection that won: "if the noise floor is 0.7, that's the headline finding." When this was finally executed for real, a gate scored identically on real data and on shuffled noise — gap 0.000 — and the output flagged itself band_near_unfalsifiable. The calibration retired the gate.

Entries 6 and 8.

Be honest about the limits

The principle was stated in 2026 and, in its first appearance, not actually run — the worked numbers beside it were placeholders. Stating a discipline and executing it are different acts, and the record is careful to distinguish them. The discipline only became real the day it cost something.

5
The honesty mechanism

Write the objection where the temptation will occur

The move: when you notice a shortcut you must not take, don't just resolve not to take it — put the prohibition in the place where a future you will be tempted. Powers are never recorded without their matching weakness.

How it shows up
  • A sample-inflation shortcut, forbidden in a plan's own first section — and the prohibition copied into the tool's help text so it can't be done by accident.
  • Disconfirming audits committed with their disconfirming numbers intact, beside the claims they undercut.
  • Every gate constant carrying the decision that moved it and the distribution that justified the move.
The cost it accepts

This makes the record harder on itself than any outside reviewer would be. That is the point — the honesty is the credibility, and it is only worth anything if it is spent on the work you are proud of, not just the work you are not.

The rule, portable

An objection recorded far from the temptation is a note. An objection recorded at the temptation is a guardrail. Put it where the hand reaches.

6
The order of thought

Analogy, then object, then formula

The move: reach for a physical or sensory picture first, convert it into a mathematical object, and only then write the equation. Never the reverse. The intuition is load-bearing; the formula is its realisation.

Entry 1 — the founding chain

Memory is listened to, like an ECG or a symphony (analogy). A difference between snapshots is a derivative of a state signal (object). Therefore ℱ{f′} = jω·ℱ{f} and the whole signal-processing toolkit applies by right (formula). Every method choice in the project descends from taking the first step literally.

Entry 1; the same order recurs at every scale, two years apart.

A tell to preserve

The sharpest statements of the intuition live in the slides, not the formal write-ups — the symphony, the zoom lens, the memory blanket. The instinct is to reach for the picture to persuade and then hide it when the writing turns formal. An agent built on this thinking should keep the picture, not suppress it.

7
The deepest one

The form is the contribution, not the realisation

The move underneath all the others: separate what any valid version must preserve from the one version you happened to build. The roles, the invariants, the conditions under which an observation means anything — that is the form. The QEMU pipeline, the Rust engine, the particular detector — those are one realisation, and disposable.

The architectural-form paper (2026) and the test it proposes

Every requirement is written three times — as a bullet, as a requirement, as polished prose — and the test for whether a sentence belongs to the form is a single question: "could a different implementation still satisfy this?" If yes, it is form. If no, it is realisation.

This is why the whole project could retreat four times — detector, then substrate, then signal validity, then form — without losing itself. Each retreat kept the form and discarded a realisation. See Entry 3.

Why this entry exists

An agent that inherits these seven moves inherits the form of the thinking, not the memory-signal realisation of it. That is the whole point of writing them down: the subject was never the transferable part. The method was.

Be honest about the limits

Seven moves extracted from one project are a hypothesis about how this person thinks, not a proven account of it. They are what the record supports so far. The test of whether they are really the method is whether they hold on the next problem — which, by the standard of this very list, is the only test that counts.

Glossary

Hover any dotted term for its definition.

Form vs realisation
The form is what any valid version of a system must preserve — its roles, invariants, and the conditions under which its output means anything. A realisation is one concrete way of building it. The test for form: could a different implementation still satisfy this sentence?
Degrees of freedom
Loosely, how much the data can actually pin down — set by the number of independent observations. A method with more free parameters than the data can constrain will fit noise, so the right complexity is bounded by the evidence, not by ambition.

The evidence
