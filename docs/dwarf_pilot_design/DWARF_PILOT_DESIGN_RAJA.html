The Dwarf Pilot, in plain words

The same four-expert verdict, retold in RAJA's voice — substance kept, jargon unpacked. The technical version lives beside this one; this is the read-it-straight-through pass.

Every fact RAJA leans on was re-checked against source: RAM 1024 MB, 500 ms interval, default substrateSpeed 2, the 128-frame streaming threshold, 4096-byte pages, and MADV_NOHUGEPAGE across every kernel family — all confirmed. Every separability number is the design's own prediction; no capture has run, so those are unverified as measurements by definition.

The spine — one sentence The thesis' founding question — “can memory writes tell you which of 13 numerical dwarfs a VM is running” — is answered no on its own terms: the 500 ms snapshot blurs 13 dwarfs down to roughly 6–8 cells of a two-axis space, and the only honest thing left to test is whether a handful of compute-heavy kernels can be slowed enough to show their motion — before the campaign either earns its budget or collapses into a confusion matrix.

Why it read hard straight: four experts each argue in their own vocabulary (sampling theory, ML leakage, ECG morphology, memory-systems ground truth) and reach the same conclusions through different doors — so the real agreements were scattered across four voices instead of stated once.

1The joint verdict

The target you were chasing does not exist; a smaller one does.

You were trying to recognize 13 dwarfs. The camera can't see them. The snapshot fires every 500 ms and ORs together every write in that half-second — a 2 Hz “stroboscope” — so thousands of compute passes smear into one flat image: “footprint fully lit.” Two of the four experts, working alone, independently threw out both “dwarf” and “family” and landed on the same replacement: a point in a two-axis space.

  • Axis 1 — the always-visible still photo: how big the footprint is, how saturated (dense-full vs sparse-stable), and the per-page magnitude regime (violent high-entropy re-seed vs quiet low-amplitude smoothing). About 3–5 shapes. You can always see this.
  • Axis 2 — the sometimes-visible motion: does the footprint sit still, grow, shrink, slide as a band, or fade. You can only see this for a few kernels, and only if you deliberately enlarge them.

So the honest deliverable is: predict the cell, and hand back the dwarf→cell confusion matrix — which is exactly what test_families_spec.md asked for in the first place. The grand target quietly became the modest one the spec already wanted.

Motion survives only where the math outruns the memory.

Rhythm is dead — nothing periodic survives a 2 Hz camera. But a one-time evolution (LU's front shrinking, QR's front growing, a solver converging) is a slow drift, not a rhythm, and drift you can rescue — if you can stretch one pass across several snapshots without the array overflowing the guest's 1 GiB RAM. The governing quantity is arithmetic intensity: flops per byte written. Work must grow faster than space.

  • QR: enlarge to ~2000, footprint ~7,600 pages, ~3% of RAM — cheap, do it first.
  • LU: enlarge to ~3,800, ~27,000 pages, ~11% of RAM — moderate, do it second.
  • DP (edit-distance), stencil, FFT: to slow them one snapshot you'd need 13.4 GB / 31.6 GB / 2.1 GB — infeasible on a 1 GiB guest, and for the stencil it's moot anyway.

The rescuable group is the dense O(N³)/O(N²) fronts (QR, LU, and by the same law Cholesky, Floyd, Smith-Waterman). Everything O(1) flops-per-byte (stencils, multigrid, nbody, LBM, FDTD, DWT) plus the memory-bound DP/FFT is permanently aliased — it folds into a “fixed footprint, no motion” bucket whose only signal is size, plus magnitude-decay for the converging ones.

Buried caveat worth lifting: the QR/LU targets come from cache-resident smoke timing. At N≈2000–4000 the working set spills out of L2/L3, so each pass actually runs slower than N³ predicts — meaning the true resize N is smaller and the rescue is stronger than the table says. But that's an estimate, not a measurement — Gate 1 exists to check it before any capture budget is spent.

The features: keep the placement-blind core, gate the address-shape extension.

  • Demoted: the founding idea — treating each page's bytes as a signal and running FFT / wavelet / cepstrum on it. The stroboscope aliases it, and default substrateSpeed 2 switches those heavy channels off. ECG even withdrew his own multigrid-cepstrum: cepstrum is the wrong tool for a monotone ramp — there's no repeating quefrency peak, so use ramp slope/curvature instead.
  • What survives (all four agree): (1) per-page magnitude distribution (hamming/l1/l2) — separates the quiet writers (n-body, barnes-hut, MD, gibbs, belief-prop) from the violent re-seeders, and is exactly what APF's binary changed/not-changed indicator throws away; (2) footprint trajectory, for resized high-intensity fronts only; (3) magnitude-envelope decay, for converging solvers whose later writes flip fewer bits.
  • Model: no autoencoder bank — it went degenerate on near-flat inputs in B1 and a forest beat it 19–59× on the same inputs. Use discriminative trees on a small feature vector.
The counting trap: effective n = number of dwarfs, not windows. A trajectory is one realization per cell; the windows inside it are points on one curve, not independent samples. A real generalization campaign therefore needs ~100–135 cells, not thousands of windows.

Schema catch: the existing change_centroid/span/runs channels are per-page (byte offsets inside one 4 KiB page), not whole-dump position. Any address-space-shape feature has to be rebuilt from the page_index column — and that column is the one most exposed to the next threat.

The deepest threat is free to test.

Kernels get their smooth address range from one mmap(), but pmemsave dumps guest-physical RAM, and the guest's buddy allocator decides where those pages actually land. Worse: every kernel calls MADV_NOHUGEPAGE, deliberately opting out of huge pages — which removes the one mechanism that would have forced physical contiguity. So the risk that changed pages scatter is higher, not lower. But the differ already emits page_index, so the test costs nothing: capture gemm at default for ~15 s at --speed 0 --sparse, take two consecutive snapshots deep in the run, and see whether the changed pages form a contiguous run or scatter across the 262,144-page range. This one check gates every address-shape claim in the pilot.

The leakage guards — what B1 taught them the hard way.

B1 got a hollow win (LORO leaked: 0.836 vs 0.457), so:

  • Split by dwarf, never by cell or rep, for any cross-motif claim.
  • The IDLE controls (spmv/bfs/mc_pi) are the tripwire: they must stay mutually inseparable at floor after masking. If they separate, you're reading the environment, not the workload.
  • Separation must survive removing absolute write volume — otherwise you just rebuilt APF.
  • For trajectories: normalize to trace-fraction (kills the duration confound) and drop warm-up snapshots (the allocation / page-fault-in transient is itself a per-workload fingerprint).
  • Normalize the capture by pass-duration, not by dimension, so footprint size can't sneak in as the label.

The pilot is one gated, cheapest-first afternoon.

Gate 0 (arithmetic, done — the table). Gate 1 (server, seconds per kernel: run kernel_qr_v2 --dim 2000 and kernel_lu_v2 --dim 3800 standalone, read the factorization counter — confirms the cache caveat before spending capture budget). Gate 2 (the contiguity capture — the single most important go/no-go in the whole pilot). Gate 3, only if Gate 2 passes: a ≥70 s capture at --speed 0 (145 frames, clears the 128-frame streaming threshold) running resized QR+LU (front migration via Jaccard overlap of consecutive changed-page sets: overlap <1 with a shifting boundary = front survived; ≈1 = still aliased), gemm as the static null, barnes_hut vs nbody as the address-shape pair, gibbs as the thinnest control (256 pages, magnitude-decay), stencil_jacobi as ECG's falsification control (the law says it stays saturated and static — if it separates, the law is wrong), dp cut to --dim 2048 (not the coded 8192 — backpressure risk), plus rmat + lexer so QR can be held out leave-one-dwarf-out.

GO resized LU/QR separate from GEMM on trajectory and barnes_hut/gibbs separate on magnitude and it all survives level-normalization, placement-invariant-only features, and warm-up dropping → commit the ~100-cell campaign.
NO-GO LU/QR still collapse into GEMM → the “front” was memorization, temporal is dead even resized, and Part 2 honestly reduces to a spatial magnitude + footprint-size classifier — deliver the confusion matrix and stop paying for the temporal arm.

The one thing they still disagree on: pilot speed.

ECG wants speed 2 (his surviving leads live there, and it matches production cadence). Memory wants speed 0 (the rich magnitude channels, the contiguity test, and a free byte-FFT A/B all need it; speed 2 is a documented throughput compromise). Resolution: run the pilot at speed 0 — cheap at pilot scale (only a few thousand changed pages), and it lets you observe axis-C, contiguity, and the byte-FFT A/B all at once — then confirm axis-C still survives at speed 2 before the campaign runs at production cadence. DSP's “free speed-0 A/B on the same retained dumps” is the bridge between the two votes.

2The four solo verdicts

Each expert's final position after the cross-examination, in his own idiom.

DSP engineer — the stroboscope

What he sees. The whole system is one filter: a 500 ms boxcar-OR, a 2 Hz stroboscope. At default sizes it smears every kernel's internal geometry into “footprint fully lit,” so the visible dwarfs collapse into ~5 write-shape archetypes — a small grid of footprint-size × magnitude buckets. Rhythm is dead, and ML is right about that. But the evolution envelope (LU shrinking, QR growing) is a DC-to-0.05 Hz trend, not a rhythm, and trends you can rescue. His one governing rule: to stretch one evolution across many snapshots while still fitting 1 GiB, work must grow faster than space. Dense linear algebra (O(N³)/O(N²)) qualifies; anything where work ≈ space folds.

What he gave up. He adopted ML's placement-invariance as a hard filter and withdrew his own “contiguous-vs-scatter shape” feature — ASLR plus guest-physical fragmentation make absolute position non-reproducible. What survives are translation-invariant stats (footprint size, run-length histogram) and temporal derivatives of spatial moments (centroid velocity, span growth-rate), which don't care where the random origin sits. He also corrected ECG: read convergence-decay on l1/l2, not hamming — a 1e-10 update still flips low mantissa bits, so hamming won't decay. And a converging solver shrinks its footprint and decays its magnitude together, which is what distinguishes it from an LU shrink at constant front magnitude — the joint (size(t), l2(t)) curve is the real discriminator.

His pilot. Two arms, both speed 2, plus a free speed-0 A/B on retained dumps. Arm A the collapse null (GEMM, stencil, hmm, diffusion — if they don't separate, archetype is the ceiling, ship the confusion matrix). Arm B the evolution rescue (GEMM/LU/QR resized + a slowed stencil — if {static, shrink, grow, decay} separate, the sub-type is real).

ML engineer — leakage and effective-n

What he sees. Neither “family” nor “dwarf” is a coherent learnable target. The real target is a point in a three-axis product: A = instantaneous footprint density (~3 levels at 2 Hz — scatter mostly collapses into dense under the boxcar-OR), B = footprint trajectory, C = per-page magnitude and its evolution. Dwarf survives only as a region — about 8–15 cells, informative for the numerical dwarfs but never reaching 13 (Jacobi, Floyd, Gibbs share a cell).

The trap he owns. Axes B and C are once-per-cell features. One LU run is one shrinking curve; the windows inside it are points on that curve, not independent samples. So your effective sample size collapses from thousands of windows to one realization per cell, and a defensible cross-motif claim needs ≥3 dwarfs × ≥3 workloads × ≥3 reps ≈ 100–135 cells. The target got richer and the data got thinner at the same moment.

This is the single most load-bearing sentence in the whole document, and it is filed as a “catch,” not the headline: the tension between a richer target and thinner data — not whether the signal exists — is what the whole program lives or dies on.

Two new leak guards. Normalize each trajectory to trace-fraction (kills the duration confound); drop warm-up snapshots (the allocation transient is a workload fingerprint — a trajectory-flavored replay of B1's floor-recognition failure). His pilot: a sized, gated growing-front generalization triad — QR, rmat, lexer (three different motifs the spec all labels SEQUENTIAL-GROW) + GEMM contrast + spmv idle-null, speed 2, leave-one-dwarf-out on the axis-B label. If the held-out growing dwarf is recognized across a motif it never trained on, surviving all three guards → GO; if it collapses → archetype is the ceiling, ship the confusion matrix. Flagged as unverified and gating: whether speed 2 actually emits per-page magnitude or only the binary indicator — if not, axis C requires speed 0.

ECG signal scientist — the arithmetic-intensity law

What he sees. The write-visible dwarfs do not carry 13 separable morphologies. After the stroboscope integrates thousands of sub-snapshot passes (his own count: 16,000–30,000 passes per snapshot at smoke sizes), the target collapses to a small set of cells on two axes — an always-present spatial image and a conditionally-present temporal trajectory. The founding byte-domain FFT/wavelet/cepstrum lens is demoted.

His law. Temporal rescue is governed by arithmetic intensity, flops per byte. High-intensity fronts (O(N³)/O(N²): LU, QR, DP, Floyd, Cholesky, Smith-Waterman) can be sized so one pass spans ≥3 snapshots under the cap; low-intensity saturators (stencils, multigrid, nbody — all O(1) flops/byte) never can, at any feasible size. He withdrew his own Phase-1 multigrid-cepstrum as impractical and conceded multigrid to ML. Cepstrum is the wrong tool for a monotone ramp anyway — use the change-centroid ramp slope/curvature; reserve cepstrum for the one genuine recurrence, the convergence-decay envelope, which he keeps.

His falsifiable move. Only the high-intensity fronts separate from GEMM on the footprint-trajectory lead; low-intensity kernels stay inseparable except by footprint size, at every feasible size. So the pilot must include a negative control: a maximally-resized stencil. His law predicts it stays aliased — if it separates, the intensity rule is wrong. Flagged discrepancy: in his smoke read, qr/lu/dp emit no pass counter and stencil shows 30,157/snap, so memory's “QR ~0.9” must come from a larger sizing — which is precisely the resizability curve, and exactly what Gate 1 resolves.

Volatile-memory engineer — the ground truth

What he sees. Aliasing isn't uniform — it's a per-kernel continuum spanning four orders of magnitude at the same cadence, which is what turns sizing-rescue into a targeted question instead of a blanket “temporal is dead.” None of these dwarfs has a volume problem — they write 2×–555× the ~118-page OS floor — but ~15 of ~20 collapse into the same footprint-magnitude bucket regardless of identity, and floor-subtraction does nothing to fix that. Speed 2 dropping the heavy channels is documented and deliberate (the --help says use speed 2–4 at 500 ms); the magnitude/texture leads need an explicit --speed 0.

His hard numbers. The per-kernel resize table turns ECG's law into RAM-bounded fact: QR ~2000 at ~3% RAM; LU ~3800 at ~11%; DP 13.4 GB and stencil 31.6 GB, both infeasible on a 1 GiB guest. The cache-effect caveat makes QR/LU's real N smaller, strengthening the rescue — but Gate 1 must confirm. He also traced two mislabels to their source: sddmm's sparsity lives in index metadata a byte-differ can't see (an inherent limit), while fem_assembly zeroes the whole matrix every pass (a fixable coding choice, not a law).

His gate. All 61 kernels opt out of transparent huge pages (MADV_NOHUGEPAGE), so physical contiguity is more exposed — but the differ already emits page_index, so a single ~15 s gemm capture at --speed 0 --sparse settles it before any address-shape feature is trusted. Vote: proceed, gates cheapest-first (0 arithmetic → 1 server timing → 2 contiguity → 3 pilot at speed 0, ≥70 s). If Gate 2 shows severe scattering, drop barnes_hut/nbody and the LU/QR-front claims, keep only magnitude + footprint-size, and make contiguity its own follow-up study.

The shape of it The honest result is already in hand — memory writes identify a cell, not a dwarf, and the confusion matrix is the guaranteed deliverable. Everything still open rides on one cheap afternoon: Gate 2's contiguity check decides whether any address-shape feature is real at all, and the resized-QR/LU vs GEMM split decides whether the temporal arm is worth a campaign. Urgent and free: run Gates 0–2. Everything downstream waits on Gate 2 passing.
